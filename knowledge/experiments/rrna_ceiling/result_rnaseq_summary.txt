Conclusion: ordinary Illumina RNA-seq (random fragmentation, size selection, random priming) gives no
nucleotide-resolution rRNA ends; a transcript truth needs end-mapping protocols (RACE, PARE, 5'/3' end sequencing,
direct RNA). Not usable as a gold standard for exact ends.
